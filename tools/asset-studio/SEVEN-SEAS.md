# Seven seas island atlas

Asset: `public/assets/islands-seven-seas-v1.webp` (1254 × 1254, transparent raster).
Generated with the built-in image generation tool; final PNG converted to WebP at quality 92 with alpha preserved.
Layout: 4 × 4 cells, pairs in order: haven, pearl coast, azure, haunted, ice, storm, abyss, lava. Draw using natural dimensions divided by four, not hardcoded 512px frames.
The approved fleet island and tower artwork is unchanged.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one production sprite atlas for a realistic isometric pirate sea game: 16 small rocky islets in a STRICT evenly spaced 4 columns by 4 rows grid, square canvas, each sprite completely isolated within its own equal square cell with 15% transparent margins. Genuine transparent background including all gutters, NO sea background, NO text, no borders, no UI. High quality detailed 3D rendered miniatures viewed from fixed elevated isometric camera, natural rock strata, tiny coves, natural arch rocks, a few tiny Mediterranean red-roof cottages and greenery. NOT vector or flat cartoon. Ordered left-to-right: row1 first2 peaceful green rocky islands with little sheltered lagoons, next2 pearl coast islands white sand and palm groves; row2 first2 azure Mediterranean rocky island villages with stone arches and peaks, next2 haunted mossy ruins in subdued ghostly mist; row3 first2 frozen rocky islands snow and pale blue ice, next2 storm weathered slate rock arches sparse grass and ruined lighthouses; row4 first2 dark abyss jagged black stone with tiny purple fissures, next2 volcanic islands with modest glowing lava streams. All small organic asymmetric landforms, avoid circular discs, no floating underside, no castles or fortress rings. Entire land and subtle shore foam inside each cell with transparent padding. Consistent scale and lighting, game-ready transparent sprites.

## Refinement prompt

Preserve all sixteen designs and their order. Shrink each island to 70% of its equal-sized cell, center it, and leave transparent gutters without clipping or overlap. Retain the square transparent canvas and realistic materials.
